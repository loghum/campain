## **Relic of the Void Seer**

_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

This cracked shard of obsidian once served as the focus of a cult leader who sought to pierce the veil between light and darkness. When held, it feels unnaturally cold, and shadows seem to bend toward it.

---

### **Properties**

While attuned to the Relic of the Void Seer, you gain the following benefits:

- You can see normally in **magical and nonmagical darkness** to a distance of 60 feet.
- You have advantage on **Wisdom (Perception)** and **Wisdom (Insight)** checks made in dim light or darkness.
- While you are in dim light or darkness, you **cannot be surprised**.

---

### **Glimpse the Unwritten (1/Day)**

When a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can use your reaction to twist fate:

- The creature must reroll the d20 and use the new result.
- You choose whether this ability benefits or hinders the creature.
