## **Cloak of Living Shadow**

_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

This shifting black cloak seems to drink in surrounding light. Its surface ripples like liquid darkness, and faint whispers can sometimes be heard when it moves.

---

### **Properties**

While wearing this cloak, you gain the following benefits:

- While you are in dim light or darkness, attack rolls against you are made with **disadvantage**.
- As a bonus action while in dim light or darkness, you can become **invisible** until the start of your next turn or until you attack, cast a spell, or move into bright light.

---

### **Call the Shadows (1/Day)**

As an action, you summon **2 Shadows** (MM p. 269) in unoccupied spaces you can see within 30 feet. The shadows act immediately after your turn and obey your verbal commands.
