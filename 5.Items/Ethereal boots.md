## Ethereal Boots  
*Wondrous Item (Boots), uncommon (attunement required)*

**Effect:**  
- The wearer of these boots can cast *Misty Step* once per long rest without expending a spell slot.  
- While wearing the boots, the wearer’s movement speed increases by 10 feet.

**Price:**  
450 gp
