**Water Crystal of the Sealing**  
_Wondrous Item, Consumable (one use)_

This shimmering crystal holds the essence of water and is imbued with the power to close portals to fiery realms.

**Properties:**

- **Activation:** As an action, you can use the crystal to attempt to close a portal to a Fire-aligned plane (such as the Elemental Plane of Fire).
    
- **Range:** 20 feet. The portal must be visible and within range.
    
- **Skill Check:** Make an **Arcana**, **Religion**, or **Nature** check. The DC is determined by the DM based on the portal’s strength.
