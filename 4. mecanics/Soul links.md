### 🩸 **Soul Link Gem Mechanics**

Each small black gem implanted in an animal (or potentially humanoid) acts as a **conduit** for a larger **Control Gem** held by the villain. The soul of the creature is bound to the gem, allowing control over their body—even post-mortem in some cases.

#### **Basic Mechanics:**

- Each soul-linked gem connects back to a **master gem** within **1 mile**.
    
- The gem allows **telepathic surveillance** and **puppet-like control** of any creature that still breathes.
    
- If the creature dies and the gem remains intact, the villain can still use the body for necromantic or ritual purposes.
    
- The gem **glows faintly** when active, pulsing in sync with the control gem.
    

#### **Detecting It:**

- **Detect Magic** shows a strong **necromancy and enchantment aura**.
    
- **Arcana DC 14** reveals the soul is partially trapped within the gem and tethered to another source.
    
- **Insight DC 12** while watching the hare reveals that its movements are not its own.
    

---

### 💠 **The Control Gem (Held by Villain)**

This gem is roughly the size of a human palm, obsidian black, and etched with infernal runes. It acts as the **central node** in a necromantic soulbinding network.

#### **Powers:**

- Can control any intact soul-link gem within 1 mile.
    
- Can **see through** any creature with an intact gem (as per _clairvoyance_).
    
- Can force a **DC 14 Charisma Save** for a creature to resist a command.
    
- Can detonate a gem, causing **psychic damage (2d6)** to the host.
    
- If a soul-linked creature is near death, the control gem can **trap the soul**, preventing resurrection unless the gem is destroyed.
    

---

### 🧿 **Ways to Break the Soul Link**

#### 1. **Physically Remove the Gem**

- Requires a **DC 15 Medicine check**.
    
- On success: The gem is extracted, the connection is broken, and the creature regains autonomy (if still alive).
    
- On failure by 5 or more: The gem shatters, and the creature takes **3d6 psychic damage** and must make a **DC 12 Con save** or fall unconscious for 1d4 hours.
    

#### 2. **Dispel Magic** (Level 3+)

- Automatically suppresses a soul link for 1 hour.
    
- Casting _Dispel Magic_ at 4th level or higher **permanently breaks** the connection to the control gem.
    

#### 3. **Destroy the Control Gem**

- Requires finding the villain and the gem.
    
- Destroying it **frees all linked souls** (alive or dead) and causes **psychic feedback**—the villain takes 5d6 psychic damage per active link.
    

#### 4. **Magic Circle + Ritual**

- Using a **Magic Circle** and special herbs (same ones smoldering in the fire) and a **DC 17 Arcana check**, the party can perform a **10-minute ritual** to sever one link.
    
- Ritual needs:
    
    - Circle of salt and powdered silver (25 gp)
        
    - At least one of the smoldering herbs found in the mill
        
    - A living creature with an intact gem
