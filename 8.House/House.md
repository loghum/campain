## Core Rule

To gain a room’s benefit:

1. Spend **3 downtime days** actively training / studying / crafting in the room.
    
2. At the end, make a relevant **Skill or Tool Check**.
    
3. On success → gain the room’s Training Boon.
    
4. On failure → you can try again after 2 more days.


|Room Stage|DC|

Advantage if:

- They roleplay cool training scenes
- They spend extra gold on materials
- They involve another PC helping


- Nat 20 → bonus lasts 1 week instead of 1 fight.
- Nat 1 → funny training accident.
