**5th-level necromancy (ritual, custom)**

- **Casting Time**: 1 minute
    
- **Range**: 60 feet
    
- **Components**: V, S, M (black iron fetter worth 500 gp)
    
- **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
    

> You bind the will of a single undead creature of CR 7 or lower within range that can hear you. The target must make a **Charisma saving throw (DC 16)** or become **charmed and magically compelled** to obey your commands as if under _Dominate Monster_, though only undead are valid targets.
> 
> While under this effect, the target cannot use Legendary Actions and treats Vaeroth as its master. If the target succeeds the save, it becomes immune to this spell for 24 hours.
> 
> If cast as part of a ritual tied to that undead’s origin (e.g., in a sealed sanctum, using true name), the save is made with **disadvantage**.
