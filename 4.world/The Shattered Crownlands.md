**Kingdom of Verdantia**

- **Government:** A stable hereditary monarchy led by King Aldric and Queen Elara. The kingdom values tradition, stewardship of nature, and prosperity through trade and diplomacy.
    
- **Economy:** Verdantia thrives on agriculture, forestry, and trade of rare herbs and crafted goods. Its fertile lands make it the “breadbasket” of the region.
    
- **Military:** Verdantia maintains a professional standing army mostly focused on defense — archers, rangers, and light cavalry adept at woodland warfare. Their focus is on protecting borders rather than conquest.
    

---

### Neighboring Countries & Relations

#### 1. **The Ironclad Marches** (To the North)

- **Government:** A militaristic confederation of city-states ruled by warlords.
    
- **Economy:** Rich in iron and minerals, known for its blacksmiths and heavy infantry.
    
- **Relations:** Historically tense with Verdantia. Frequent border skirmishes over mining rights and trade routes. A fragile truce exists after a recent peace treaty mediated by a neutral third party.
    
- **Current Status:** On edge but not officially at war. Possible sparks if either side pushes too hard.
    

#### 2. **The Azure Dominion** (To the East)

- **Government:** A mercantile oligarchy ruled by wealthy merchant families and guilds.
    
- **Economy:** Thrives on trade, naval power, and luxury goods. Controls key ports and sea routes.
    
- **Relations:** Mostly friendly with Verdantia. They import Verdantian herbs and exports exotic goods in return. There’s mutual respect but underlying competition over influence.
    
- **Current Status:** Peaceful with active trade agreements; some political intrigue as merchant families vie for dominance.
    

#### 3. **The Shadowlands** (To the South)

- **Government:** A dark and secretive theocracy ruled by a cabal of priests devoted to shadowy deities.
    
- **Economy:** Focuses on arcane secrets, forbidden magic, and mercenary services.
    
- **Relations:** Hostile. Verdantia fears their growing power and occult experiments. Border raids and disappearances are common.
    
- **Current Status:** Cold war with increasing border tensions; Verdantia bolsters defenses.
    

#### 4. **The Free Cities of Vandar** (To the West)

- **Government:** A loose alliance of independent city-states with elected councils.
    
- **Economy:** Diverse, with emphasis on crafts, magic, and mercenaries. Known for harboring exiles and adventurers.
    
- **Relations:** Neutral but cooperative. Many Verdantians seek mercenaries or arcane knowledge here. Occasional disputes over refugees or trade rights.
    
- **Current Status:** Peaceful, with occasional diplomatic negotiations.


### Timeline of Major Events (Last 100 Years, up to 1344)

**1244**

- **Founding of the Kingdom of Verdantia**  
    The unified Kingdom of Verdantia was established when King Eadric the Bold united several warring human clans under one banner. The kingdom’s capital city, Verdant Haven, was built in the fertile heart of the vale.
    

**1259**

- **The Ironclad Marches’ First Incursion**  
    A coalition of warlords from the Ironclad Marches launched a raid into Verdantia’s northern border, aiming to seize control of the mineral-rich foothills. The conflict was repelled, but tensions rose sharply.
    

**1274**

- **The Treaty of Greenroot**  
    Verdantia and the Ironclad Marches signed a fragile peace treaty ending two decades of border skirmishes. The treaty established neutral zones and trade routes but did not fully resolve the underlying conflicts.
    

**1284**

- **Rise of the Azure Dominion**  
    Several merchant families united the coastal city-states east of Verdantia, forming the Azure Dominion. They rapidly expanded their naval power and trade influence, challenging Verdantia’s regional dominance.
    

**1294**

- **The Shadowlands’ Dark Ascendancy**  
    A mysterious cult within the Shadowlands seized power, establishing a theocratic regime devoted to shadow deities. Dark magic and forbidden rituals spread, and occasional raids into Verdantia’s southern border began.
    

**1304**

- **The Siege of Verdant Haven**  
    Shadowlands forces launched their first major assault on Verdant Haven, attempting to destabilize the kingdom. The city withstood the siege thanks to the king’s leadership and the valor of the Verdant Guard.
    

**1314**

- **Founding of the Free Cities of Vandar**  
    A coalition of independent city-states to the west formed an alliance, creating a haven for exiles, adventurers, and merchants. They became an important trade partner but sometimes harbor fugitives from Verdantia.
    

**1324**

- **Trade War Between Verdantia and Azure Dominion**  
    Economic rivalry erupted over control of rare herbs and luxury goods. Both kingdoms engaged in sabotage, espionage, and proxy conflicts on the high seas.
    

**1329**

- **The Ironclad Marches Break the Truce**  
    A warlord faction rejected the Treaty of Greenroot and restarted border raids. Verdantia mobilized its forces but avoided full-scale war through diplomatic efforts.
    

**1334**

- **Rise of Internal Nobles Pushing for Expansion**  
    Several noble houses in Verdantia began advocating for aggressive policies towards the Shadowlands and Ironclad Marches, increasing political tension at court.
    

**1339**

- **Shadow Cultists Infiltrate Verdantia**  
    Reports of disappearances and strange occurrences in southern villages point to deep infiltration by Shadowlands cultists, hinting at a coming conflict.
    

**1344 (Present Day)**

- Verdantia stands at a crossroads: war with the Ironclad Marches looms, trade tensions with the Azure Dominion simmer, and the Shadowlands threaten from the south. The Free Cities of Vandar remain wary but could be powerful allies or wild cards in the coming storms.
