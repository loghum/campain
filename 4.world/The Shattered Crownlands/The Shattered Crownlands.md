### **1. Thal'Zaruun** – _The Shattered Spires_

**Race(s):** Predominantly elves (wood & high), with pockets of gnomes and fae creatures  
**Theme:** Mystical, ancient, nature-bound, arcane-focused  
**Description:**  
Thal'Zaruun is a land of massive stone spires covered in ivy and magic-infused trees. Its cities are carved into cliffs or hidden in enchanted groves. The people are isolationist, valuing harmony with nature and the preservation of ancient magic. Verdantians often see them as aloof or arrogant.

**Notable Cities & Towns:**

- **Cael'Thalor** – Capital; a city grown from living crystal and wood, hidden behind illusions
    
- **Virelen Hollow** – A trading outpost on the edge of a great enchanted forest
    
- **Luneval Spire** – A towering mage-city devoted to the study of celestial magic
    
- **Mistmere** – A fog-covered lakeside town where fae sometimes walk among mortals
    
- **Brambleshade** – A quiet village near a druidic circle, shrouded in twilight

### **2. Skarnholm** – _The Stonebound Dominion_

**Race(s):** Primarily dwarves and dragonborn, with hardy halflings and humans in mining towns  
**Theme:** Rugged, mountainous, industrious, militarized  
**Description:**  
Skarnholm lies across jagged peaks and deep chasms, rich with minerals and ancient forges. It’s a kingdom of honor, labor, and iron. Its people are stoic and tough, often clashing with Verdantia over resources or border disputes. The terrain makes it hard to invade—and hard to leave.

**Notable Cities & Towns:**

- **Drakvar Hold** – Capital; a fortified city carved into a mountain, ruled by a High Thane
    
- **Frostglen** – A mining town in the northern peaks, often snow-covered
    
- **Anvildeep** – An ancient forge-city run by master smiths and artificers
    
- **Stonehook** – A cliffside port where Skarnholm trades its ore for food and cloth
    
- **Cinderrun** – A volcanic town where obsidian and lava glass are mined
