### **Countering Kalthar Expansion**

- Kalthar arrived first, staking claims in the northwest with mines and military outposts.
    
- Verdantians view Kalthar as a **direct ideological and practical threat**—a nation that hates magic and could expand to threaten Verdantia itself.
    
- Establishing colonies in the southeast gives Verdantia a foothold on the island to **monitor and counter Kalthar**, creating a natural buffer zone.
    

### 2. **Rumors of Magical Resources**

- Lizardfolk spoke of **magic trees** deep in the interior—trees whose wood can be used to craft staves and wands that amplify spells.
    
- Verdantians are curious and opportunistic: having access to such rare resources would **enhance their magical power** and give them an edge over Kalthar.
    

### 3. **Trade and Exploration**

- Southeast Xathliss offers rivers, fertile swamplands, and coastal access.
    
- Verdantians hoped to **trade with local tribes** and harvest natural resources (herbs, rare woods, and minerals) in a way that fits their magical culture.
    

### 4. **Strategic Outposts**

- Colonies on Xathliss are **stepping stones for further exploration**—a chance to establish magical research stations and safe harbors in an otherwise dangerous region.
    
- The island is large and largely unknown in the interior, making it ideal for **experiments and exploration without interference** from rival nations.

## ⚖️ **Consequences**

- **Initial goodwill**: Lizardfolk saw Verdantians as allies against the Kalthar, sharing knowledge of the land.
    
- **Overreach**: Verdantians expanded farther than promised, claiming sacred swamps, mountains, and riverways.
    
- **Tension**: Some tribes occasionally attack settlements or caravans, especially in the swamps and mountains that remain lizardfolk strongholds.
