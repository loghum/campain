## 🌍 **Geography & Landscape**

- **Fertile heartlands**: Verdantia is green, lush, full of magical farmland maintained by druids and mages.
    
- **Crystal rivers**: Flow with faint magical energy, used to power mills, enchantments, and magical experiments.
    
- **Arcane cities**: Built with floating spires, magically lit streets, and wards woven into the walls.
    
- **Sacred groves**: Druids protect forests where ley lines are strong, and where fey or elementals sometimes slip into the world.
    

---

## 🧑‍🎓 **Culture & Society**

- **Magic is life**: Where Kalthar sees blackpowder as progress, Verdantia sees magic as the natural path of civilization.
    
- **Arcane education**: Even common folk may know a cantrip or two, taught in village schools. Advanced study happens at great colleges.
    
- **Art & architecture**: Buildings often incorporate magical effects (floating gardens, walls that glow with runes, enchanted fountains).
    
- **Everyday enchantments**:
    
    - Farmers use spells for better harvests.
        
    - Healers use divine and arcane magic alike.
        
    - Transportation is aided by teleport circles or magically guided ships.
        

---

## 🏛️ **Politics & Governance**

- **Council of Magisters**: A ruling body of powerful wizards, druids, sorcerers, and clerics who represent different magical traditions.
    
- **Loose confederation**: Verdantia isn’t a centralized empire, but a coalition of semi-independent city-states bound by magical treaties.
    
- **Major cities**:
    
    - **Elaris** – “The Floating City,” capital of the Council.
        
    - **Thrymere** – Known for its druidic circles and agriculture.
        
    - **Veythallion** – A port city, famous for magical shipwrights and artificers.
        

---

## 🛡️ **Military**

- **Mage-Knights**: Warriors trained in both martial combat and magic.
    
- **Battlecasters**: Wizard regiments that fight in disciplined formations.
    
- **Summoned allies**: Elementals and constructs are often bound to fight alongside armies.
    
- **Fleet**: Verdantia’s navy is augmented with enchanted ships that sail faster than wind, and some even have bound elementals for propulsion.
    

---

## 🧩 **Religion**

- Unlike Kalthar, divine casters thrive here.
    
- **Pantheon of Light & Balance** is widely worshiped, but each city tends to have patron gods.
    
- **Mysticism**: Some see the Weave itself as divine, worshiping magic as a living force.
    

---

## ⚔️ **Relations with Other Nations**

- **With Kalthar**: Bitter ideological enemies. Verdantians see them as _fearful_, _small-minded_, and _dangerous_ for banning magic.
    
- **With Xathliss**: Complicated. Verdantia has **colonies in the southeast** of the lizardfolk lands. Some Verdantians believe in diplomacy and learning from the lizardfolk’s spiritual ways; others see them only as obstacles to magical expansion.
