### 🕯️ **Vaeroth-Kael, the Hollow Flame – Appearance**

Vaeroth-Kael is a frail but imposing figure, cloaked in layers of tattered ceremonial robes that once belonged to a royal academy’s archmage. The once-deep indigo threads are now scorched and moth-eaten, smoldering faintly at the hems as if they remember fire. His gaunt frame moves with unnatural grace—puppet-like and twitching—revealing no breath or warmth.

His **face is a half-mask of bone**, cracked and seared to his skull, leaving one **milky eye glowing with cold green light** while the other is a **sunken, scorched socket**. Faint trails of black ichor leak from his lips and eye as if his own magic is trying to escape him. His hands are skeletal and shrouded in pale fire—**not hot, but hungering**, like frostbite masquerading as flame.

An eerie **aura of silence and dry wind** follows him, curling pages, snuffing candles, and chilling the air. Around his neck hangs a **twisted metal amulet**, pulsing faintly with runes—the key to binding Sir Caldrath. His voice is soft but hoarse, like parchment tearing:

> _"I gave my soul to carve open death, and now it gnaws back..."_
