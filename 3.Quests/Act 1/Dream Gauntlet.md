# ⚔️ DREAM GAUNTLET – 3 DEADLY FIGHTS (NO DOPPELGANGERS)

---

## 🧪 FIGHT ONE: **“The Monster Below”**

> _Muffled rumbling shakes the dream-arena. “It feeds beneath the earth...”_

### 🧱 Arena

- Cracked stone, with shallow pits and half-destroyed cover.
    
- Dreamlike shadows writhe at the edge of vision.
    

### 🧟 Enemies

- **1 Bulette** (CR 5) – Ambush predator, high damage, AoE with deadly leap.
    
- **1 Grick Alpha** (CR 7 but toned down to ~CR 5 by reducing HP to ~80) – Tentacle ambusher with resistance to nonmagical weapons.
    
- **1 Spellcasting Hobgoblin Devastator** (CR 4, MM p.187) – Focus fireball, _Scorching Ray_, AoE threat.


```encounter
name: Encounter name
creatures: 
  - Bulette
  - Grick Alpha
  - Hobgoblin Devastator
  - 
```

🧠 **Tactics**

- Bulette ambushes the backline from underground.
    
- Grick Alpha climbs walls/ceiling for surprise tentacle strike.
    
- Hobgoblin stays at range and softens the party with **Fireball** (possibly on round 2).
    

✅ **What it tests**

- Handling melee pressure and AoE magic
    
- Split-field threats
    
- Resource burn via burst damage

## 🧠 FIGHT TWO: **“Voices of the Grave”**

> _“Sing, child,” whispers a voice. “Let the pain in.”_

### 🧱 Arena

- A shadow cathedral. Floating candles provide light; extinguishing one creates a magical darkness sphere (10 ft).
    
- Whispering echoes confuse perception.
    

### 🧙‍♂️ Enemies

- **1 Banshee** (CR 4) – _Wail_, _Horrifying Visage_
    
- **1 Cult Fanatic** (CR 2) – _Hold Person_, _Spiritual Weapon_, _Command_
    
- **2 Meazels** (CR 3, MM p.218) – Teleporting, grappling assassins with disease
    
- **2 Shadows** (CR 1/2) – Sap STR and hide in darkness
    
```encounter
name: Encounter name
creatures: 
  - 2: Cult Fanatic
  - 2: Shadow
  - 2: Meazel
  - Banshee
  - Arcane Adept
  - 
  - 
  - 
```
🧠 **Tactics**

- Shadows and Meazels lurk in darkness; teleport and ambush casters.
    
- Banshee floats in and uses _Wail_ to drop weakened PCs.
    
- Cult Fanatic buffers from a safe corner, uses **Spiritual Weapon** and **Command** to disrupt.
    

✅ **What it tests**

- Wisdom saves vs. _Wail_, _Hold Person_, _Visage_
    
- Magical darkness tactics
    
- Casters under pressure
    
- Healing and support coordination

## 💀 FIGHT THREE (BOSS): **“The Dream Tyrant Ascends”**

> _The air hums. Time flickers. A golden figure descends, cloaked in stars._

### 🧱 Arena

- Crystalline platform floating in void
    
- Shifting gravity zones (Dex Save or fall prone at initiative 20)
    

### 🧙‍♂️ Enemies:

- **1 Githyanki Knight** (CR 8, MM p.160) – Remove _Plane Shift_; this is the "Dream Tyrant"
    
- **1 Lamia** (CR 4, MM p.201) – Wisdom saves, charm, illusion magic, claws
    
- **1 Helmed Horror** (CR 4, MM p.183) – Antimagic field immune, flies, magic-resistant
    
```encounter
name: Encounter name
creatures: 
  - Arcane adep
  - Githyanki Knight
  - Helmed Horror
```
🧠 **Tactics**

- The **Githyanki** uses _Telekinesis_, _Misty Step_, and the **Dream Suppression Aura**:
    
    > _(Creatures within 10 ft of him can't take reactions or bonus actions.)_
    
- The **Lamia** targets spellcasters with **suggestion** or **mirror image**, or claws up the Artificer.
    
- The **Helmed Horror** engages the frontline, immune to many magic effects.
    

✅ **What it tests**

- Boss fight coordination
    
- Anti-magic & resistance play
    
- High burst + battlefield control
    
- Sorcerer/Artificer spell choice under pressure
