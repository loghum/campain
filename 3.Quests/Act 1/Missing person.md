## 🕯️ **Case 1: The Butcher’s Boy – Emrik**

**Name:** Emrik  
**Age:** 14  
**Status:** Alive, hiding in fear  
**Last Seen:** Leaving the butcher’s shop near dusk, delivering a meat package to the temple  
**Connection:** Emrik was chosen for a soul link ritual but escaped when it failed

### 🧭 **How the Party Finds Him:**

- **Villagers mention** that Emrik **never delivered his package**.
    
- Speaking to **the butcher (Tomasz)** reveals that Emrik used a **shortcut through back alleys**.
    
    - 🧠 **Investigation DC 12**: Detect torn meat wrappings, scuffed shoe marks, and smeared chalk symbols leading to the next location.
        
    - 👣 **Survival DC 12**: Track torn meat wrappings and scuffed marks in the alley.
        

### 📍 **Location:**

An **old storage cellar** in the back of a supply shop, long unused.

### 🕵️ **Scene:**

- Inside: **Dust, cobwebs**, and an old **ritual circle** on the ground — smudged and broken.
    
- **Burned herbs** in a brazier, the air smells slightly sweet and bitter.
    
- A **cracked black gem** lies inert on the floor — no longer functional.
    
- **Behind crates**, the party finds **Emrik**, frightened but alive, silent unless gently approached.
    
    - 🧪 **Medicine DC 13**: Check Emrik’s condition — physical health is fine, but mental trauma is evident.
        
    - 🧠 **Insight DC 14**: Sense Emrik’s fear; his hands are trembling.
        

> _“He lit the fire and told me not to move... but the stone broke. He yelled, and I ran.”_

## 🌲 **Case 2: The Herbalist – Marelle**

**Name:** Marelle  
**Age:** 53  
**Status:** Dead, ritual failed  
**Last Seen:** Walking toward the forest to gather rare herbs  
**Connection:** Lured for a ritual by promises of unlocking hidden knowledge and healing magic

### 🧭 **How the Party Finds Her:**

- Her home contains a **marked foraging map** with a **path circled in red**.
    
- Villagers say she always returned before nightfall — the path is well-known.
    
    - 🧠 **Investigation DC 11**: Find the foraging map in her house with a red-circled path.
        
    - 👣 **Survival DC 11**: Track her footprints leading off-trail into the forest, to a **clearing deeper in the woods**.
        
    - 🧪 **Arcana DC 12**: Detect traces of magical energy leading away from the cleared path.
        

### 📍 **Location:**

A hidden **forest glade** about 40 minutes’ walk from the village.

### 🕵️ **Scene:**

- A **ritual circle** of scorched stones, half-collapsed.
    
- A **charred scroll fragment** clings to a nearby log.
    
- **Marelle’s body** lies nearby — peaceful expression, no wounds.
    
- A **cracked black gem** is embedded in tree roots, pulsing faintly.
    
    - 🧠 **Arcana DC 14**: Confirm that this was an attempt to **bind Marelle’s soul** to the gem, which failed and caused her **psychic death**.
        
    - 🧪 **Medicine DC 13**: Determine that Marelle died from **psychic shock** rather than physical injury.
        

> _Optional Hook_: The soul of **Marelle** might still be trapped inside the gem — a potential future quest to release her soul.

## 🏚️ **Case 3: The Granger Family – Combat Encounter**

**Names:**

- **Roff** (father), **Dala** (mother)
    
- **Lias** (10) and **Ketta** (6)  
    **Status:** Alive, but under soul link control  
    **Last Seen:** Heading home from the market at sunset two nights ago  
    **Connection:** The entire family was taken for an **experimental group binding**.
    

### 🧭 **How the Party Finds Them:**

- Visiting the **Granger home** reveals:
    
    - Dinner on the table, **untouched**.
        
    - **Footprints leading out the back**.
        
    - A **doll on the bed** — with a tiny black gem embedded in its forehead.
        
        - 🧠 **Investigation DC 12**: Inspect the doll and the house for clues; the gem is identical to those in the other cases.
            
        - 👣 **Survival DC 13**: Follow the trail through nearby wheat fields, leading to an **old orchard clearing** about **20–25 minutes** away.
            
        - 🧪 **Arcana DC 14**: Recognize that this gem is another soul-binding link, similar to Marelle’s.
            

### 📍 **Location:**

An **overgrown orchard** near a stream, just beyond the village boundary.

### 🕵️ **Scene:**

- The family stands motionless under a **withered apple tree**, eyes glassy, completely unaware of their surroundings.
    
- **Nyx**, the tiefling henchman, watches from the shadows — grinning, arms crossed.
    
    - 🧠 **Insight DC 14**: Sense that Nyx is **gloating**, enjoying the party’s confusion.
        
    - 🧪 **Arcana DC 15**: Feel the **magical energy** radiating from the black gems in the family members’ foreheads.
        
    - 🧠 **Persuasion DC 16**: Attempt to convince Nyx to release the family (fails unless he is caught or the gems are removed).
