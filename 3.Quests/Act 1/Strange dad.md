Zarnibus and Bibble believe that by using the magic potato, they can **transform themselves into potatoes**—not because they’re seeking power, immortality, or any sort of grand magical achievement, but because they **think it’s the ultimate form of magical enlightenment**. They believe that by becoming potatoes, they will transcend the mundane world of wizards and **achieve pure, untapped potential**.

They reason that:

1. **Potatoes are the most magical of all vegetables**: Zarnibus and Bibble have spent years studying the properties of plants and vegetables, and they’ve come to the conclusion that **potatoes** are the key to unlocking true enlightenment. After all, potatoes can be mashed, baked, fried, or boiled—**endlessly versatile**, which to them represents endless **possibility**.
    
2. **Potatoes are the perfect form of existence**: Being a potato means no more stress, no more worries, and no more meddling in the affairs of mortals. They think that as potatoes, they can **live in peace** in the soil, free from the burden of magic and the pressures of wizard life. Plus, who would want to mess with a potato? **Potatoes are unbothered.**
    
3. **The potato’s humble nature is the secret to ultimate power**: Zarnibus and Bibble believe that true power lies in humility. **Potatoes are humble**—they grow in the dirt, quietly offering their sustenance without seeking attention. They believe that by turning into potatoes, they will **unlock the most potent and pure form of magic**, untethered from the pride and egos of wizardry.
    
4. **No more complicated spells**: After years of experimenting with complicated spells, the wizards have decided that being a potato is **the easiest magic** of all. They don’t need to worry about weaving intricate incantations or focusing on difficult ingredients—**just turn into a potato** and enjoy the simplicity of being.
    
5. **Potatoes can’t be outdone**: Zarnibus and Bibble believe that no one can defeat a potato. They think that if they can turn into magical potatoes, they will be **immune to curses**, **immune to attacks**, and **immune to anything at all**. After all, no one can truly defeat a potato—they’re too ordinary to be noticed and too tough to be destroyed!
