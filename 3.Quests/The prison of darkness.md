# 🏰 What Is the Prison?

The Prison of Darkness is an underground arcane facility built by an ancient order to contain a powerful shadow entity:

**The Umbral Warden** — a sentient being of living darkness that feeds on fear, memory, and light.

It was bound centuries ago using magical seals powered by light-based relics. Those seals are now failing.

---

# ⚠️ What’s Happening Now?

A cult called **The Veil Unbound** has infiltrated the ruins. They believe releasing the entity will grant them power and “free the world from the tyranny of light.”

They’ve:

- Broken some containment seals
- Corrupted parts of the prison
- Accidentally unleashed lesser shadow creatures

The prison is now unstable—both physically and magically.
