## 🧩 Puzzle: _The Labyrinth of Right Intention_

### **Setup**

The players enter a stone corridor that forks repeatedly—left and right—into a seemingly endless maze. Inscriptions and faded murals line the walls, each depicting trials of moral judgment, deception, and trust. A glowing inscription appears as they step in:

> _“Only those who walk the path of right may find the truth. The right path is always the right one.”_

### **Mechanics**

The maze is magical and recursive. Every time the players reach a fork, they are presented with a left or right path.

- **If they choose RIGHT**: They proceed safely and the environment subtly changes (lighting, wall carvings, whispers)—a clear signal they are progressing.
    
- **If they choose LEFT**: They end up **back at the beginning**, though with a slight alteration (e.g., one torch is missing, or a shadow of themselves walks past them in reverse), to hint that time or reality is distorted.
    

After **five correct right turns**, they arrive at the exit chamber or the next phase of the dungeon.

#### Left Path – Class Trial Room (Solo Scene)

- Player is teleported to a stone chamber. A glowing scroll hovers above a pedestal.
    
- Voice echoes: _“What do you serve — power, purpose, or protection? Speak truly, or be judged for falsehood.”_
    

##### Mechanics:

- Present a class-based riddle (see examples below)
    
- Player gives a spoken answer aloud. No checks.
    

**If answer is thematically true or character-driven:**

- The scroll opens. Light surrounds the player.
    
- They are teleported forward to rejoin the main path.
    

**If answer is dishonest or deeply off-theme:**

- Scroll shatters. The chamber dims.
    
- Player makes a **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw**.
    
    - **On failure**: Gain **1 level of [[Exhaustion]]**.
        
    - **On success**: No exhaustion, but delayed 1 turn before returning.

### **Optional Twists**

- If the party keeps going left, a **maze wraith** or **echo of failure** appears and begins harassing them.
    
- A time limit: after a set number of wrong choices (e.g., 4 resets), the maze **starts to collapse**, forcing them to choose wisely or flee.

>Aan het einde staan twee standbeelden: één huilend, de ander streng en geblinddoekt.

>In de muren zijn ooit prachtige muurschilderingen uitgehouwen, nu besmeurd met zwarte handafdrukken en overschilderd met arcane tekens. Je voelt je bekeken, niet door iets aanwezigs... maar door herinneringen.

- **Perception (DC 12):** The weeping statue's eyes were once made of silver, now gouged out. The other’s shield has a broken corner.
    
- **Religion (DC 15):** These figures are known as _Thandrel, the Just_ and _Liora, the Merciful_, patrons of an ancient paladin order.
    
- **Arcana (DC 14):** The corruption here is laced with necrotic magic but cannot suppress the lingering divine presence. This room resists full desecration.

Each statue has a mechanism:

- **Judgment** (Thandrel): Offers a riddle involving fairness or consequence.

- **Mercy** (Liora): Offers a riddle involving compassion or redemption.

The party must solve both **and** choose a moral answer that aligns the spirits.

Example Riddles:

- **Thandrel**:

_“What punishes without cruelty, teaches without hate, and brings balance to the wicked?”_

_(Answer: Justice)_

- **Liora**:

_“What saves the guilty and the innocent alike, but leaves none unchanged?”_

_(Answer: Mercy)_

**Final Decision Prompt:**

After solving the riddles, the spirits ask:

> _“A knight steals bread to feed his starving child. Do you punish, forgive, or guide? answer at once.”_

The party must **agree** on an answer. If they reach consensus, the spirits grant divine favor. If they argue or take extremes, the statues seal shut.

**Success Result:** Unlocks a rune with a divine symbol etched onto it needed to unlock the final seal holding Caldrath.

**Failure Result:** Seal remains partially intact, making Caldrath harder to awaken later.

### 🔹 5. **The Forgotten Sanctum (Boss Room)**

They enter a wide, obsidian chamber.

- In the center: **Sir Caldrath**, asleep in an open sarcophagus, his soul visibly draining toward a floating black reliquary.

- Floating nearby: **Selvara**, magically restrained and glowing faintly.

- Standing between them: **Vaeroth-Kael**, protected by an **arcane ward** maintained by two cloaked ritual sentries.

- Around the room: **undead adventurers**, mutated and half-mindless.

---

## **Boss Encounter Structure**

### Phase 0 – Setup

### 📣 **Vaeroth Speaks (Telepathically or Projected Magic)**

> _(As the players hear a chorus of voices overlapping Vaeroth’s true voice)_  
> **“Fools. You’ve undone what even time dared not touch.”**

Players can act before combat if fast:

- Precast spells

- Free Selvara. She's unconscious unless healed (Arcana DC 15).

### Phase 1 – Ritual Sentries & Minions

- Sentries (CR 3 mages or wights) maintain the ward.

- When both are defeated, the **arcane ward collapses**.

```encounter

ame: Ritual Sentries

creatures:

- 2:Graveborn Skirmisher
- 2:Ghoul Minion
- Bonecrush Sentinel

```

```encounter

ame: Ritual Sentries

creatures:

- Lair

```

**Each round on initiative 20**, one Acolyte can:

- **Use Channel Ward** (Recharge 6): grant 10 temp HP to a Skirmisher or Bonecrush Sentinel
    
- Or **Chill Bolt** once per round (as a low-damage warning shot)

### Phase 2 – Vaeroth Joins the Fight

- He casts _Bind the Fallen Will_ targeting Caldrath.

- The reliquary pulses; Caldrath begins to twitch.

```encounter

name: Vaeroth-Kael

creatures:

- Vaeroth-Kael

- 2:Dark Acolyte

```

### Phase 3 – Caldrath Awakens

- If ritual was interrupted: **Caldrath resists**, joins the party.

### 💥 The Awakening Scene: _The Jedi Grab_

> As Vaeroth’s spell falters and the sanctum begins to tremble, Caldrath stumbles forward, dragging half-broken chains. His voice, like thunder over a funeral pyre, utters a name lost to time:

> **“Griefpiercer... come.”**

> The holy blade trembles in its resting place, then **rockets across the chamber**, spinning end-over-end through broken stone and dust. It slams into Caldrath’s outstretched hand as **holy fire erupts down the length of the blade**, lighting his skeletal form with golden flame.

> “Now… _we end this._”

- If not: **Caldrath rises bound**, fights as a controlled death knight.

Ways to help him resist:

- Destroy the reliquary (AC 17, HP 25).

- Present relics from the Hall of Vigil.

- Use Selvara’s voice or blood to sever the link.

```encounter

name: The Hollow Flame & The Sleeping Knight

creatures:

- Vaeroth-Kael

- Sir Caldrath Doomspire  


```

---

## **Possible Outcomes**

- **Caldrath breaks free** and helps defeat Vaeroth.

- **Vaeroth succeeds**, becomes a full lich, escapes or is fought at full power.

- **Selvara dies**, the bloodline ends, and Caldrath is lost forever.

---

## **Treasure**

- [[Knight’s Oathblade]] – Sentient blade tied to Caldrath’s past

- [[Locket of Selvara]] – Grants protection against necromantic magic

- Scroll of _Greater Restoration_, 300 GP in artifacts, 1 rare magical item tied to a paladin’s legacy

## **Sequel Hooks**

- The **Obsidian Ledger** arrives after the battle, asking unsettling questions.

- Selvara begins dreaming of Caldrath’s final days.

- One of the party members hears Caldrath’s voice in their dreams: _“You freed me... but not the others.”_
