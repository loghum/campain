### 🎭 **Phase 1: The Ritual**

**Encounter Mood:** Tense, eerie anticipation.

- **Scene:** Party enters the sanctum to find Vaeroth mid-ritual, channeling _Bind the Fallen Will_ into Sir Caldrath, who is bound in magical chains or runes.
    
- **Enemies:**
    
    - 1x **Vaeroth-Kael (CR 8)** – casting, but not attacking yet
        
    - 2x **Undead Minions (CR 2 each)** – guardians
        
    - 1x **Turned Adventurer (CR 2)** – twisted mockery of a former hero
        
- **Vaeroth is shielded** until a ward is disrupted (e.g., via Arcana check or killing both minions).
    
- **Party goal:** Stop or disrupt the ritual before it completes.
    
- **Optional Skill Challenge:** 3 successes (Arcana, Religion, Insight) before 2 failures = Vaeroth loses control of Caldrath early.
    

---

### 💀 **Phase 2: The Broken Chains**

**Encounter Mood:** Chaos, high stakes.

- If ritual is disrupted or fails, **Caldrath breaks free**.
    
- He is **confused and hostile** to everyone for 1 round (fighting both sides), then **turns on Vaeroth**.
    
- **Combat Intensifies:**
    
    - Vaeroth becomes active: casts _Blight_, _Graveburst_, etc.
        
    - **Undead reinforcements (CR 1 each, like skeleton knights)** arrive from side tunnels: 2 per round for 2 rounds.
        
- **Twist Possibility:** Vaeroth sacrifices a minion to regain HP, fueling desperation.
    
- **Party goal:** Survive the chaos, fight smart, and protect Caldrath or convince him.
    

---

### 🧨 **Phase 3: Hollow Flame’s Fall**

**Encounter Mood:** Climax, desperate magic.

- Vaeroth, now wounded, goes **invisible or teleports** to reposition.
    
- He unleashes **signature spell**: _Bind the Fallen Will_ again—maybe targeting a PC or Caldrath.
    
- **Legendary Resistance used here** to avoid CC and raise tension.
    
- **Caldrath and party coordinate attacks**, possibly with **Caldrath tanking**, giving party time to finish the job.
## 🏰 LAIR ACTIONS: _The Forgotten Sanctum of Sir Doomspire_

_Once the death knight begins to awaken, his ancient influence stirs the dungeon itself..._

> **Initiative Count 20 (losing initiative ties):** While Vaeroth is in control, the Sanctum is unstable, resisting both undead and living alike. Once Caldrath breaks free, these lair actions begin to reflect **his will returning**.

---

### 📜 **Pre-Reclamation (Vaeroth controls the sanctum):**

Choose one per round:

- **1. Echoes of Binding:**  
    Glowing runes flare. One creature of Vaeroth’s choice must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or be **restrained** by spectral chains until the start of the next round.
    
- **2. Necrotic Pulse:**  
    Dark energy erupts from the ritual circle. All non-undead creatures take **6 (1d12) necrotic damage** (DC 14 Con save for half).
    
- **3. Memory of the Dead:**  
    Whispers from the fallen soldiers sap confidence. One creature within 60 ft. must make a **DC 15 Charisma save** or suffer **disadvantage on its next attack or save**.
    

---

### 🛡️ **Post-Reclamation (Caldrath reasserts control):**

Once Caldrath breaks Vaeroth’s spell or the ritual is disrupted:

- **1. Vengeful Flame:**  
    The sconces blaze with ghostly blue fire. Up to two undead of Vaeroth’s choice **take 10 radiant damage** and **lose resistance to radiant damage** until the end of their next turn.
    
- **2. Walls Remember Their Master:**  
    A **10-ft section of wall collapses** on a target within 30 ft. of a chosen point (Dex save DC 14 or take 2d10 bludgeoning and be **knocked prone**).
    
- **3. Doomspire’s Will:**  
    One PC may **reroll a failed saving throw** from that round as Doomspire’s protective magic shields them (1 use per PC per combat).
    

---

## ⚙️ Integration Tips:

- Swap from Vaeroth's to Caldrath’s lair actions **as soon as Caldrath regains partial control** (narrative milestone).
    
- Keep these actions **supportive but not overpowering**—they're meant to shift the tone and raise tension, not decide the battle.
    
- You can signal the transition with **visual effects**: chains breaking, cold air vanishing, torches reigniting.
