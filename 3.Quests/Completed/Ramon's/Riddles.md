### 🪓 **Barbarian Riddle – Rage and Control**
**Riddle 1:
> _"I burn hotter than fire, yet leave no ash.  
> I blind the wise and strengthen the weak.  
> I can save you or doom you—  
> But only if you fail to master me."_

🧠 **Answer**: **Rage**

**Riddle 2: “You are cornered. Unarmed. A child screams. Your blood burns. Do you think — or do you charge?”**

**Answer:** I charge.

> _But the real trick is to prompt reflection: Is instinct always right?_

> Optional twist: The "right" answer in the maze might be **“I think”**, depending on the moral of the test.

 **Riddle 3: “I am the echo of the mountain. The howl of the blood. I move before thought, strike before plan. What guides me?”**

**Answer:** Instinct

> _Could be misled by choices like 'Anger' or 'Revenge'._

### 🛡️ **Paladin Riddle – Oath and Conviction**

**Riddle 1:
> _"I bind with no chain, but cannot be broken.  
> I am sworn, not spoken.  
> Betray me, and you betray yourself.  
> What am I?"_

🧠 **Answer**: **Oath**

**Riddle 2: “You swore an oath. Your god commands death. Your heart cries mercy. Which voice do you follow?”**

> **Correct Answer:** My conscience — oaths must serve justice

- Wrong: My god always, I follow orders, The law above all
    

**Riddle 3: “A blade forged in truth cuts lies. But it can also cut the innocent. Do you still wield it?”**

> **Correct Answer:** Only when guided by wisdom  
> _Perfect for Oath of Redemption or Devotion paladins._

### ⚙️ **Artificer Riddle – Creation and Ingenuity**

**Riddle 1:
> _"Born not of nature, nor of spell—  
> I live in sparks, in steam, in gears as well.  
> I do not think, but I obey,  
> And without me, your plans decay."_

🧠 **Answer**: **Invention** or **Machine**

**Riddle 2: “I create what gods cannot. I give life without breath, thought without soul. What am I?”**

> **Answer:** An artificer

- Wrong: Necromancer, God, Golem
    

**Riddle 3: “Your machine can save a village or destroy a kingdom. One button. No undo. What do you build?”**

> **Correct Answer:** One that requires choice, not a trigger  
> _Tests the player's understanding of moral implications of invention._

### 🔥 **Sorcerer Riddle – Innate Magic and Chaos**

**Riddle 1:
> _"I came with your birth, not forged by your hand.  
> I dance through your blood, wild and unplanned.  
> I need no scroll, no study, no rite—  
> Yet I shape the stars and shatter the night."_

🧠 **Answer**: **Magic** or **Sorcery**

**Riddle 2: “Power came before you knew your name. It sings in your blood. But who controls whom?”**

> **Answer:** I do — power obeys the will

- Wrong options: The power controls, The blood, Destiny
    

**Riddle 3: “A spell not studied, a power not earned. Are you the master, or merely the vessel?”**

> **Answer:** The master — mastery lies in choice, not origin  
> _This distinguishes sorcerers from warlocks or wizards in a deep, identity-based way._
