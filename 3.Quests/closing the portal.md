- **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana / Investigation):** You learn **the portal is weakly stable** but will widen if left unchecked.
    
- **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana / History / Investigation):** You discover **legends of a Marid who possesses a crystal capable of sealing the portal**.
    
- **DC 18 Intelligence / Arcana (rare):** You locate **precise instructions**:
    
    - The crystal can **absorb the portal’s energy**.
        
    - Must be **activated in the portal’s vicinity**.
        
    - **Marid requires a personal exchange** before releasing it.

Books are writen by [[Jorvan Trask]]

### **Marid’s Offer**

- They **will not hand over the crystal for free**. Possible exchanges:
    
    1. **An item of power** (weapon, gem, artifact the party carries)
        
    2. **A service or quest** (e.g., slay a rival elemental, bring a rare ingredient)
        
    3. **A secret or favor** (information only the party has, e.g., knowledge of another portal)
        
- They are **negotiable**, but picky. Marid likes clever, bold, or tricky characters.
    

### **Social Checks**

- **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion):** Marid explains **the crystal exists but is difficult to retrieve**.
    
- **DC 16 Persuasion / Insight:** The party learns **exactly what the Marid wants**.
    
- **DC 18 Deception / Persuasion:** Party can try to **trick the Marid** (risky; failure may anger them).
