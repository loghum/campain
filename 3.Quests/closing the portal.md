# 🌊 **The Broken Pact**

**Level:** 5  
**Length:** 1 session (~3–4 hours)  
**Themes:** Poison, corruption, broken oaths, purification

---
### **Setup**

At the **Water Temple**, the **marid** guards the **Water Crystal**, needed to seal a planar rift.  
He refuses to give it up until a **broken pact** is restored.

> Er rijst iets op uit het water, verwrongen en onstabiel. Zijn huid glanst in ziekelijk blauw en groen, donkere aderen trekken zich kronkelend over zijn lichaam, en zijn ogen gloeien met een stormachtig, onnatuurlijk licht. Als het spreekt, klinkt de stem schor en scherp, als golven die over rotsen slaan.

> _“My currents are fouled. The scaled ones let their waters rot. Restore the bond, or your world will drown in filth.”_

He gestures toward the marsh beyond the temple — home to a tribe of lizardfolk once bound to him by oath.

## 🐊 Scene 1 – The Lizardfolk Village

**Boxed Intro:**

> A thick, green fog clings to the swamp. The air tastes of mold and stagnant water. Wooden huts rise on stilts, surrounded by sluggish pools where fish float belly-up.

The tribe greets the party with suspicion but not violence.

**NPCs:**

- **Shaman Vasskath**, gaunt and weary, clutching a **Flowstone Amulet** streaked with dark green veins.
    
- **Chief Kressk**, proud and defensive of his people.
    
- **Acolyte Ssiva**, young and still faithful to the marid.
    

**What they learn:**  
The tribe’s sacred spring has become poisoned from below.  
Vasskath tried to purify it by invoking an **ancient swamp spirit** — but it twisted the ritual, corrupting the water and breaking the pact.

**Skill checks:**

- **Arcana / Nature (DC 14):** The poison is magical and spreading unnaturally fast.
    
- **Persuasion (DC 13):** Convince Vasskath to guide the party to the source.
    
- **Insight (DC 12):** He blames himself but hides that he awakened something dangerous.
    

> **Vasskath:** “It called itself _the Eater Below_. I thought it would heal the waters. Instead, it drank them dry.”

He agrees to lead them to the poisoned spring.

## 💠 Scene 3 – Restoring the Pact

**Boxed Intro:**

> Back at the Water Temple, the marid looms over a basin of shimmering water, the air heavy with pressure like a coming storm. The Flowstone Amulet hums faintly as it’s lowered into the pool.

The marid demands a ritual of purification before he releases the crystal.

**Skill Challenge (2 successes before 1 failure):**

- **Arcana / Nature (DC 14):** Guide the flow of cleansing energy.
    
- **Religion (DC 13):** Invoke the words of the original oath.
    
- **Persuasion (DC 13):** Convince the marid to forgive the shaman’s failure.
    

**Success:**  
The basin glows with blue light. The marid’s anger fades.  
He grants the **Water Crystal** and blesses the party.

> _“The poison is gone. You have my current’s gratitude — and my crystal.”_

**Failure:**  
The ritual works, but the marid is unimpressed.  
He still gives the crystal but declares, _“Our tides will cross again, mortals.”_
