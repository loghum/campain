
### 📍 1. **Collapsed Surface Ruin (Entrance)**

- Overgrown ruins of a forgotten temple.
    
- Entrance in front of a broken altar.
    
- Wild undead or cursed wildlife roam here.
    

---

### 📍 2. **Hall of Vigil (Fallen Paladins’ Tombs)**

- Rows of broken sarcophagi and desecrated murals.
    
- Holy symbols defiled with necrotic sigils.
    
- One or two paladin ghosts linger, offering cryptic warnings or a side quest.
    
## 🧩 ***Mechanics:***

1. **Statue Interaction Puzzle:**  
    Each statue has a mechanism:
    
    - **Judgment** (Thandrel): Offers a riddle involving fairness or consequence.
        
    - **Mercy** (Liora): Offers a riddle involving compassion or redemption.
        
    
    The party must solve both **and** choose a moral answer that aligns the spirits.
    
    Example Riddles:
    
    - **Thandrel**:  
        _“What punishes without cruelty, teaches without hate, and brings balance to the wicked?”_  
        _(Answer: Justice)_
        
    - **Liora**:  
        _“What saves the guilty and the innocent alike, but leaves none unchanged?”_  
        _(Answer: Mercy)_
        
2. **Final Decision Prompt:**  
    After solving the riddles, the spirits ask a question:
    
    > _“A knight steals bread to feed his starving child. Do you punish, forgive, or guide?”_
    
    The party must **choose an answer**—if they argue and reach consensus, the spirits grant the relic. If they fight or pick extremes, the statues seal shut.

### 📍 3. **Vault of Chains**

- A wide, circular chamber where the lichling binds souls.
    
- Soulsteel chains hang from the ceiling.
    
- Arcane device channels magic toward the lower sanctum.
    
- **Combat Zone:** The party may interrupt a ritual or fight Vaeroth's undead constructs here.
    

---

### 📍 4. **Hall of Echoes**

- Narrow corridors with whispering spirits; DC 15 WIS save or gain minor madness.
    
- Echoes of ancient battles—illusions or ghostly reenactments.
    
- Contains puzzle locks or divine ward runes barring deeper access.
    

---

### 📍 5. **Vaeroth-Kael’s Study**

- Rotting scrolls, preserved brains in jars, a half-complete phylactery.
    
- Spellcasting notes about the ritual and the death knight.
    
- **Optional Encounter:** Vaeroth may appear here briefly, speaking through a skull or magical illusion.
    

---

### 📍 6. **The Forgotten Sanctum (Boss Arena)**

- Large, domed chamber with an obsidian sarcophagus at its center.
    
- Broken stained-glass murals depict Sir Caldrath’s fall from grace.
    
- Magic circle etched in ash and bone.
    
- **Final Battle Setup**:
    
    - Vaeroth is completing the ritual.
        
    - Interrupting it may weaken or delay the death knight’s awakening.
        
    - Failing to stop it in time triggers a two-phase fight: Lichling + weakened Death Knight.
        

---

### 🗝️ Secret Areas (Optional Additions)

- **Hidden Reliquary**: Sealed room containing a holy weapon or artifact that Caldrath once wielded.
    
- **Collapsed Passage**: Leads to a deeper, forgotten vault that could serve as a sequel dungeon or lair expansion.
