_Uncommon wondrous item (ring), requires attunement_

> A ring of black iron shaped like a **cracked signet**, its band etched with faint runes resembling shackles. It's always cold to the touch. When worn, it hums softly in places where death still lingers—but offers protection, not malice.

---

### ✨ **While wearing this ring, you gain the following benefits:**

- **Ward of the Fallen**:  
    When you are reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, you can drop to **1 HP instead**.  
    Once used, this effect cannot trigger again until **the next dawn**.
    
- **Death’s Memory**:  
    You have **advantage on saving throws against being frightened by undead**.
    
- **Shadowmark** _(Flavor Effect)_:  
    In dim light, your shadow sometimes **lags behind your movements**, as if watched by another.  
    This effect fades **if you speak Caldrath’s name aloud**.
