> _“Forged in the final days of the Silver Requiem, this blade drank the blood of traitors and demons alike. When Doomspire fell, it refused to be claimed by evil—and waited.”_

---

name: Griefpiercer (Inert)
type: Weapon (longsword)
rarity: Unique
requires_attunement: No 
properties:
  - Weapon Type: Longsword (1d8 slashing / versatile 1d10)
  - Magic Weapon: Counts as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistances
  - Faint Pulse: Emits dim light in a 5 ft. radius
  - Holy Recoil: Whenever the wielder strikes an undead creature, roll a d6:
      - On a 6, the blade lashes out with radiant energy, dealing an additional 1d8 radiant damage.
      - On a 1, the blade rejects the strike — the wielder takes 1d4 radiant damage.
flavor:
  - The blade is heavy, as though resisting its own use. It thrums faintly with power, but **feels incomplete** in mortal hands.
  - When touched, the runes flicker but do not glow. Any attempt to attune fails silently.


### 📖 Description (as discovered by the party):

The sword rests in a stone pedestal, half-buried in rubble and ash in a side chapel. It radiates a **faint pulse of warm light**, its hilt designed like **spread angelic wings**, with a crossguard that resembles **a broken crown**. The blade itself is long and elegant, with **runic inlays that flicker only when touched by good-aligned hands**.

> When a PC approaches:
> 
> - The room grows still.
>     
> - Whispers of past battles echo faintly.
>     
> - Any undead nearby instinctively recoil.
>     

**Engraving on the blade’s fuller:**  
_“In silence, I waited. In wrath, I return.”_

### 🔱 Mechanical Notes (if a PC tries to wield it before):

- **Requires attunement by a Good-aligned creature** or **Caldrath himself**.
    
- Without Caldrath: acts as a **+1 longsword** with minor radiant flashes (flavor).
    
- After Caldrath reclaims it: upgrades to a **+2 greatsword** with radiant smite (or homebrew undead-slaying ability if he fights alongside the party).
