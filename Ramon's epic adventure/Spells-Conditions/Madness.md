## Madness Effects

**DC 15 WIS save**

Madness can be short-term, long-term, or indefinite. Most relatively mundane effects impose short-term madness, which lasts for just a few minutes. More horrific effects or cumulative effects can result in long-term or indefinite madness.  
  
A character afflicted with **short-term madness** is subjected to an effect from the Short-Term Madness table for 1d10 minutes.  
  
A character afflicted with **long-term madness** is subjected to an effect from the Long-Term Madness table for 1d10 × 10 hours.  
  
A character afflicted with **indefinite madness** gains a new character flaw from the Indefinite Madness table that lasts until cured.  
  

Short-Term Madness
|d100|Effect (lasts 1d10 minutes)|
|---|---|
|01–20|The character retreats into his or her mind and becomes [paralyzed](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_8). The effect ends if the character takes any [damage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_46).|
|21–30|The character becomes [incapacitated](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_6) and spends the duration screaming, laughing, or weeping.|
|31–40|The character becomes [frightened](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_4) and must use his or her [action](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_20) and [movement](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Movement?expansion=34047#content) each round to flee from the source of the fear.|
|41–50|The character begins babbling and is incapable of normal speech or [spellcasting](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Spells?expansion=34047#toc_1).|
|51–60|The character must use his or her [action](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_20) each round to [attack](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_21) the nearest creature.|
|61–70|The character experiences vivid hallucinations and has [disadvantage](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_2) on [ability checks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Ability%20Scores?expansion=34047#toc_4).|
|71–75|The character does whatever anyone tells him or her to do that isn’t obviously self-­destructive.|
|76–80|The character experiences an overpowering urge to eat something strange such as dirt, slime, or offal.|
|81–90|The character is [stunned](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_13).|
|91–100|The character falls [unconscious](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_14).|
