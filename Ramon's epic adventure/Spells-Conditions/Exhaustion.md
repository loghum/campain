In D&D 5e, exhaustion is a condition that represents severe physical or mental fatigue, measured in six levels. Each level imposes increasingly debilitating effects, and they are cumulative, meaning the effects of all previous levels are added together. 

Here's a breakdown of the levels and their effects: 

- **Level 1:** Disadvantage on ability checks.
- **Level 2:** Speed is halved.
- **Level 3:** Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws.
- **Level 4:** Hit point maximum is halved.
- **Level 5:** Speed is reduced to 0.
- **Level 6:** Death.
