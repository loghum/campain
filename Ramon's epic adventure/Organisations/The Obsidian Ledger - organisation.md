
**Overview:**  
The Obsidian Ledger is a covert division within the kingdom’s Arcane Ministry (or your world’s equivalent magical authority), tasked with the cataloging, containment, and—when necessary—confiscation of powerful magical artifacts and unexplored dungeons. Officially, it doesn’t exist. Its operatives work in shadow, often disguised as scholars, diplomats, or even adventurers.

**Purpose:**  
To monitor arcane threats to national stability by maintaining a hidden “ledger” of all known magical relics, sites of power, dungeon entrances, and extraplanar portals. They prevent dangerous knowledge or items from falling into the wrong hands—sometimes including the crown’s rivals or even other branches of the same government.

---

### **Structure:**

- **The Auditor-General** – The mysterious head of the Ledger, whose true identity is unknown. Only top agents receive direct communication from them.
    
- **Surveyors** – Field agents who scout and report on magical sites, often posing as adventurers or independent scholars.
    
- **Scribes** – Magical archivists who inscribe findings into encrypted tomes hidden in “black vaults.”
    
- **Silencers** – Enforcers who clean up evidence, silence leaks, or remove rogue mages when needed.
    

---

### **Methods:**

- Employing magical surveillance, scrying rituals, and arcane marks to trace artifacts.
    
- Creating false maps, curses, or illusions to mislead unapproved explorers.
    
- Quietly funding or manipulating adventuring parties to retrieve items or test dungeon defenses without revealing the Ledger’s involvement.
