## 🌊 **Vaelis, the Drowned Whisper**

### **Basic Overview**

- **Type:** Marid (Fey/Elemental Water Genie)
    
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral (leans toward benevolent curiosity, but unpredictable)
    
- **Current State:** Bound to an ancient temple’s fountain deep underground — his essence trapped in crystal-clear water that never runs dry.
    
- **Goal:** To experience the mortal world once more through his new Warlock; to one day _feel rain on his skin_ and _taste salt on the wind_ again.
    

---

## 💠 **Appearance**

Vaelis manifests as a towering, elegant being of **living water** that constantly shifts between shapes — sometimes humanoid, sometimes a coiling serpent of translucent blue. His face is beautiful but androgynous, his voice echoing like it’s spoken from beneath deep waves. When calm, his body is glass-clear; when angered, it churns with bubbles and dark currents.

> _“The sea remembers everything, mortal. Even the names of those who tried to drown it.”_

---

## 🎭 **Personality**

|Trait|Description|
|---|---|
|**Curiosity**|Fascinated by mortal emotion — love, fear, joy — and wants to feel them through his Warlock.|
|**Whimsy**|Often speaks in riddles or poetic contradictions. His moods shift like tides.|
|**Pride**|He remembers being a prince among the marids; demands respect, though not obedience.|
|**Loneliness**|The temple is his cage. He hides his sorrow behind grandeur.|
|**Temper**|Rare but terrifying — when truly enraged, his voice becomes thunder underwater.|

---

## 🗣️ **Speech Style**

- Speaks in **measured, melodic tones** that ebb and flow like waves.
    
- Prefers **metaphor and emotion** to direct instruction.
    
- Calls mortals by poetic epithets: _“Child of breath,” “Little river,” “Fleeting spark.”_
    

**Example Phrases:**

- “The world above shifts like foam, but beneath, the current endures.”
    
- “Bring me the taste of rain, little river. Let me dream of storms again.”
    
- “You call it freedom — I call it the wind’s lie.”
    
- “The pact is no prison. It is a current. Swim with it… or drown.”
    

---

## ⚓ **How He Interacts with the Party**

|Situation|Behavior|
|---|---|
|**First Meeting**|Grand but weary. He speaks as though to a court, yet clearly suffers from isolation.|
|**After First Task**|His tone softens toward the party. To the future Warlock, he shows particular interest, as if recognizing potential.|
|**When Offering the Pact**|Calm, intimate, almost whispering — his words feel like water brushing skin. He doesn’t demand, he _invites_.|
|**When Angered or Betrayed**|The room floods with phantom water, his voice turning into crashing surf. “You forget whose tide carries you.”|

---

## 💧 **Connection with the Warlock**

Once the pact is made:

- Vaelis can **see the world through the Warlock’s senses** — he may comment on sensations (_“Ah, warmth… I had forgotten”_) or emotions (_“Your anger feels clean, like a storm breaking”_).
    
- He may appear in **reflections, pools, or dreams**, sometimes gentle, sometimes imposing.
    
- When the Warlock casts certain spells (_Fog Cloud, Misty Step, Control Water_), the faint echo of Vaelis’s voice can be heard.
    

---

## 🔮 **Future Hooks**

- **The Key to Freedom:** Vaelis’s temple binds him with a forgotten sigil. The Warlock might one day learn how to break it… at a cost.
    
- **Jealous Spirits:** Other marids, or perhaps elemental rivals (efreet or dao), may view his pact as a dangerous breach.
    
- **Shared Fate:** As Vaelis’s essence intertwines with the Warlock’s, the line between them begins to blur — dreams become memories, emotions overlap.
