A jovial human man in his late 40s with a bushy mustache and a round belly, Marek earned his nickname from his love of collecting shiny trinkets, which decorate the pub’s walls. He is known for his loud laugh, quick wit, and fondness for storytelling. Marek was once a sailor and uses his maritime experience to flavor the pub's atmosphere, including a hand-carved ship's wheel behind the bar.
