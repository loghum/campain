### **Traveler’s Beacon**

_Wondrous item, rare_

This small, rune-inscribed stone is warm to the touch and hums with latent magical energy. Once attuned, the bearer can use it to return to a predetermined location.

**Teleportation Ritual.** As an action, the user can activate the _Traveler’s Beacon_ to begin a teleportation ritual. The ritual takes **5 minutes per person** being teleported, up to a maximum of **10 creatures** (50 minutes total). At the end of the ritual, all chosen creatures within **10 feet** of the beacon are teleported to a previously designated safe location, such as a home, guild hall, or temple. This location must be a place the user has attuned the beacon to by spending **1 hour in meditation** there. The beacon can only be attuned to one location at a time.

**Limitations.** The beacon cannot teleport unwilling creatures. If the ritual is interrupted before completion, no teleportation occurs, and the magic is not expended. The beacon can be used **once per long rest**.
