### Flameheart Dagger
*Weapon (Dagger), uncommon (attunement required)*

- **Damage**: 1d4 piercing damage
- **Properties**: Finesse, light
- **Effect**: The Flameheart Dagger deals an additional **1d4 fire damage** on a hit. Once per day, the wielder can use a bonus action to ignite the blade, causing it to deal an additional **2d4 fire damage** for 1 minute.
- **Attunement**: Requires attunement by a character proficient with daggers.
- **Price**: **500 gp**
