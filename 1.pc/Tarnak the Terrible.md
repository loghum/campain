|d10|Effect|
|---|---|
|**1**|A chaotic feedback loop of energy surges through your body. You and all creatures within 10 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, take **1d8 lightning damage** and are **pushed 10 feet** away from you. On a success, take half damage and aren't pushed.You can trigger this chaotic feedback again as a **bonus action** on each of your turns while raging
|**2**|Each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take **1d12 necrotic damage**. You gain **1d12 + your Barbarian level** temporary hit points.|
|**3**|You teleport up to **30 feet** to an unoccupied space you can see. Until your rage ends, you can use this effect again as a **bonus action on each of your turns**.|
|**4**|A flumph or pixie spirit appears next to a creature of your choice within 30 feet. At the end of your turn, it explodes: the creature must make a Dexterity save or take **1d6 force damage**. You can summon one each turn as a **bonus action** while raging.|
|**5**|One weapon you're holding becomes infused with magic. Until your rage ends, its damage type becomes **force**, and it gains the **light and thrown** properties (range 20/60). If already thrown, range increases by 20 feet.|
|**6**|Whenever a creature hits you with an attack roll before your rage ends, it takes **1d6 force damage** from magical backlash.|
|**7**|You are surrounded by shimmering, multicolored lights. You and allies within 10 feet gain **+1 AC** until your rage ends.|
|**8**|Vines and flowers erupt around you. For the duration of your rage, the ground within 15 feet is **difficult terrain** for enemies.|
|**9**|A bolt of radiant light shoots from your chest at a creature you can see within 30 feet. It must make a Constitution save or take **2d8 radiant damage** and be **blinded until the start of your next turn**. You can use this again as a **bonus action** each turn while raging.|
|**10**|Your rage warps the flow of time. Until your rage ends, you can **reroll one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw** **once per turn**, but must use the new result.|
