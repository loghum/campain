### **Locket of Self-Reliance**

_Wondrous Item, uncommon (attunement required)_

**Effect:**  
When you fail an ability check or saving throw, you can use your reaction to reroll the check or save, but you must accept the second roll. This can be used once per long rest.

**Price:** 600 gp
