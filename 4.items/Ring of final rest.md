### Ring of the Final Rest

_Wondrous Item (Ring), rare (no attunement required)_

- **Effect**: When the wearer is reduced to **0 hit points**, they **die instantly** instead of falling unconscious. They **cannot be stabilized or revived** by normal means, including _Revivify_ or _Raise Dead_. Only a _Wish_ spell or divine intervention can restore them to life.
- **Curse**: Once worn, the ring **cannot be removed** unless the wearer willingly takes it off or dies. A creature who knowingly puts on this ring feels an eerie sense of finality.
