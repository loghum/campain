### 🌲 **Amulet of the Grovebound**

_Uncommon, requires attunement_

This amulet is made from a braided strand of silvervine and bark, bound around a polished stone etched with a symbol of the ancient grove. It hums softly when near natural life and falls silent in the presence of corruption.

---

**Properties:**

- **Grove’s Blessing.** If the amulet is **buried in the heart of a sacred grove**, it purges the area of dark magic, **cleansing corruption** and banishing minor undead or blight-related effects within a 60-foot radius. The amulet is consumed in the process.
    
- **Nature’s Veil.** While worn and attuned, **undead beasts and undead monstrosities** (such as zombie wolves, undead stags, or other reanimated forest creatures) **ignore the wearer** unless directly harmed or provoked.
