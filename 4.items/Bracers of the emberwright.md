### **Bracers of the Emberwright**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (W)
    
- **Weight:** 1 lb
    
- **Rarity:** Homebrew
    
- **Attunement:** Required
    
- **Value:** 1,800 gp
    

---

**Description:**  
Forged by ancient dwarven smith-mages, these rune-etched bracers glow faintly when channeling fire magic or tinkering with tools.

**Effects:**

- You gain a **+1 bonus to spell attack rolls** made using **Artificer spells**.
    
- You gain a **+1 bonus to checks made with Tinker’s Tools and Smith’s Tools**.
    
- **Once per short rest**, when casting a spell that deals **fire damage**, you can **reroll one damage die**.
    

**Modifier:**

- Spell Attack +1 (Bonus)
