# 💎 Water Crystal
*Wondrous Item, Legendary (requires attunement by a spellcaster or someone who can channel elemental energy)*

---

## Description
A swirling blue crystal that feels cool to the touch, as if tiny waves are trapped inside. It hums near water or elemental disturbances.

---

## Properties

- **Elemental Focus:** Once per long rest, you can cast *Create or Destroy Water*. If you are level 5+, you can also cast *Control Water*.

- **Portal Seal:** To close a portal connected to the water or fire planes, the crystal must be **placed into the portal itself and held there for 10 minutes**. Anyone holding it must make a **DC 15 Arcana check** every 5 minutes to maintain the connection. On a failure, the portal resists, producing a minor water surge (2d10 damage to all within 10 ft.).

- **Ocean’s Boon:** While attuned, you can breathe underwater and gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.
