### **Girdle of the Stoneblood Clan**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (W)
    
- **Weight:** 2 lbs
    
- **Rarity:** Homebrew
    
- **Attunement:** Required
    
- **Value:** 2,500 gp
    

---

**Description:**  
A broad dwarven belt engraved with the runes of the Stoneblood Clan.

**Effects:**

- Your Strength score increases by 1, to a maximum of 21.
    
- While raging, you gain resistance to **force damage**.
    
- Once per long rest, if you drop below 25% HP while raging, you gain **10 temporary hit points**.
    

**Modifier:**

- Strength +1 (Ability Modifier)
