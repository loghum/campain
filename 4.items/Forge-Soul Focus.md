### **Forge-Soul Focus**

- **Type:** Focus (R)
    
- **Weight:** 1 lb
    
- **Rarity:** Homebrew
    
- **Attunement:** Required
    
- **Value:** 1,500 gp
    

---

**Description:**  
A focus made from obsidian fused with glowing forge veins.

**Effects:**

- You gain a **+1 bonus to spell attack rolls**.
    
- **Once per short rest**, a fire spell you cast can **ignore fire resistance**.
    
- You can cast **Control Flames** **once per day**.
    

**Modifier:**

- Spell Attack +1 (Bonus)
