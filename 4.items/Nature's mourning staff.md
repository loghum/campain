### **Nature’s Mourning Staff**

_Uncommon, requires attunement (by a druid, ranger, or nature-themed spellcaster)_  
_Weapon (quarterstaff)_

This quarterstaff is carved from the heartwood of a fallen elder tree and etched with ghostly images of animal spirits. It hums with quiet sorrow and pulses with life when danger draws near.

---

**Properties:**

- **Weapon Type:** Quarterstaff (versatile 1d6/1d8 bludgeoning)
    
- **Magical Effect:**
    
    - **Nature’s Grasp.** You can cast the _Entangle_ spell once per long rest. (Save DC 13)
        
    - **Forest Wisdom.** You gain a +1 bonus to Nature checks while in forest terrain.
